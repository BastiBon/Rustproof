Last updated: [DD.MM.YYYY]

## 2. Principle: Your data stays on your device

Rustproof stores all entries you create (e.g. activities, point values, targets, penalty rules, plans and history) exclusively on your device. As the developer, I have no access to this data. The app does not require a user account and does not transfer your entries to me or to third parties.

If you uninstall the app or delete the app data, this data is removed from your device. You can back up your data beforehand using the export function.

## 3. Download via Google Play

A Google account is required to download the app. Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, processes data under its own responsibility (e.g. account data, device data, time of download). I have no influence on this. More information: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

Google provides me with aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. number of installs, crash rates, ratings).

## 5. Notifications

The app can show you local reminders, e.g. when an inactivity limit is reached. These reminders are generated on your device; no data is sent to a server. You can switch off notifications at any time in your device settings.

## 6. Step data via Health Connect

Step sync is switched off by default. Only when you actively switch it on in the app does the app ask for permission to read your step count via the Android interface Health Connect. The app only requests read permission for step data. Each time you open the app, the steps of the last 7 days are read and recorded as an activity. The data is processed exclusively on your device and is not transferred to me or to third parties.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by switching on step sync and granting access in Health Connect. You can withdraw it at any time by switching off step sync in the app or revoking the permission in the Health Connect settings.

## 7. Contact by email

If you send me an email, e.g. with a question or a feature request, I process your email address and the content of your message in order to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I use Gmail by Google for the mailbox. I delete your message once it has been dealt with and no retention obligation applies.

## 8. Suggesting features and voting

Via the menu item "Suggest a feature" in the app or via the website, you can reach a feedback board (<https://rustproof-app.sleekplan.app/feedback>) where you can suggest new features, vote on suggestions and report bugs. The board opens in your browser; the app itself does not transfer any data.

The board is operated by Sleekplan GmbH, Leopoldstraße 31, 80802 Munich, Germany. When you use it, the data you provide there is processed, e.g. username, email address and profile picture, your suggestions, comments and votes, as well as technical data such as browser and operating system version, times of your activity and an anonymised location derived from your IP address. Use is voluntary. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in developing the app further based on its users' wishes (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), or your consent where the provider obtains it (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Sleekplan. The data is stored with Amazon Web Services in Ireland (EU). Where Sleekplan's sub-processors process data outside the EU, this is done, according to Sleekplan, on the basis of EU Standard Contractual Clauses. More information: <https://sleekplan.com/custom/privacy_enduser>

## 9. Storage period

Your entries in the app are stored only on your device and remain there until you delete them or uninstall the app. I delete emails and posts on the feedback board once they have been dealt with or if you ask me to, unless a statutory retention obligation applies.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future. As your entries are stored only on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly in the app or via your device settings. For anything else, email me at [rustproof.app@gmail.com](mailto:rustproof.app@gmail.com).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. [supervisory authority of your German federal state].
