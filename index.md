# Rustproof

**Nobody is perfect every day – and nobody has to be.**

Most trackers only reward the perfect streak: miss one day, and you start over.
Rustproof sees it differently. It's not about completing every activity on every
single day, but about building consistency: sticking with it on most days and
doing things again and again. A rest day is fine – you're allowed to take one.

**How it works:** You assign points to your activities – sports, housework, work
or anything that matters to you – and set yourself a daily target. You collect
points by checking off activities. If something is left undone, Rustproof
reminds you. If you keep letting it slide, you may get minus points. You set all
the rules yourself – whatever you need to keep from getting rusty. Charts show
you how consistent you really are over weeks and months. If you like, Rustproof
also counts your steps, and you can track values such as weight, personal bests
and other measures as well.

In the basic version, your data stays on your phone only. Backup or sync is
planned for later as an option that you switch on yourself.

Rustproof is built by a single developer, and your ideas decide what comes next.
